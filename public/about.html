Our Story

Built in Kigali.
Worn everywhere.

DOTTIE.YZ emerged from the intersection of Rwandan street culture and global fashion. We design for those who move differently.

Est. 2026

From the streets of Kigali to the world

We started with a simple belief: streetwear should be more than just oversized graphics. It should carry the weight of culture, the precision of craft, and the confidence of someone who knows exactly who they are.

Every DOTTIE.YZ piece is designed in Kigali, informed by the energy of the city — from the murals of Kiyovu to the beats playing in downtown studios. We work with premium fabrics, precision cuts, and a design language that speaks to the modern creative.

This isn't a trend. This is the code.

What Drives Us

Design Philosophy

Precision Craft

Every seam, every stitch, every print position is deliberate. Premium heavyweight fabrics, reinforced construction, and finishes that last beyond the season.

Global Identity

Rooted in Kigali, connected to the world. Our designs bridge local authenticity with international appeal — streetwear that translates across borders.

Creative Freedom

No templates. No safe choices. Every collection is an experiment in form, texture, and silhouette. We push boundaries because conformity is the opposite of style.

We are not a trend.
We are not a movement.
We are the code.
Quality & Craftsmanship

Engineered to endure

We source heavyweight French terry, ring-spun cotton, and custom knit fabrics. Our garments are pre-shrunk, enzyme-washed, and double-stitched at stress points.

Screen-printed graphics use plastisol and water-based inks that survive 50+ washes. Zippers, drawcords, and hardware are specified for durability — because premium means forever, not for the season.
