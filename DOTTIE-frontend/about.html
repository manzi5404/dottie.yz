Our Story

Our Conviction

DOTTIE.YZ isn't just a label; it's a declaration. Born in the streets of Rwanda and designed for the global movement, we bridge the gap between spiritual conviction and streetwear culture.

Every piece carries a message. Every stitch represents a stand. We don't just make clothing; we create armor for the bold, the believers, and the brave.

The Quality

Every piece is engineered for longevity and comfort. From our 400GSM heavyweight hoodies to our precision-cut essential tees, we prioritize the fabric that carries our message.

Designed in Kigali. Crafted for the world. DOTTIE.YZ represents more than fashion; it's a mindset that refuses to be silenced.
